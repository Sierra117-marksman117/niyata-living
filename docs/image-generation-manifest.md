# Image Generation & Production Manifest: Niyata Living

This manifest documents every generated image asset, prompt foundation, geometry consistency verification, file size targets, and review status for the Niyata Living contemporary furniture reference build.

---

## 1. Quality & Consistency Assurance

### Product Identity Preservation Rules
1. **Silhouette & Proportions**: Every product maintain exact dimension ratios, leg taper, backrest curvature, and cushion volume across hero, side, top, detail, and room lookbook angles.
2. **Material Honesty**: Teakwood exhibits straight, warm golden-brown grain with silky matte oil reflection. Dholpur sandstone exhibits subtle honed beige quartz mineral texture. Brass exhibits directional satin brushed luster.
3. **Believable Indian Proportions**: Room settings represent realistic 1 BHK (Mumbai), 2/3 BHK (Bengaluru/Ahmedabad), and urban high-rise (Surat) layouts with standard 9.5–10.5 ft ceiling heights and functional circulation pathways.
4. **No Artificial Artifacts**: Strictly zero brand logos, watermarks, deformed geometry, floating legs, or AI text artifacts.
